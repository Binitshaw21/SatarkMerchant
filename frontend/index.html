The Attack Vector

The "Paper Blindspot" of India's UPI Boom

While consumers scan QRs to make payments, small merchants have zero technical tools to verify their physical countertop stand hasn't been tampered with.

01

Physical Hijack

Bad actors print custom QR stickers with identical dimensions and discreetly slap them over the merchant's authentic code during peak counter rushes.

02

Silent Revenue Drain

Customers scan the fraudulent code and the payment goes through instantly—but to the scammer's bank account instead of the shopkeeper's.

03

Zero-Hardware Audit

SatarkMerchant eliminates expensive IoT biometric sensors. Any merchant can use their basic smartphone camera to audit their stand in two clicks.
