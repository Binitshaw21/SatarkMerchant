The Attack Vector

The "Paper Blindspot" of India's UPI Boom

While consumers scan QRs, small merchants have zero tools to verify their stand hasn't been tampered with.

01

Physical Hijack

Bad actors print custom QR stickers with identical dimensions and discreetly slap them over the authentic code during peak counter rushes.

ATTACK_TYPE: PHYSICAL_SUBSTITUTION
02

Silent Revenue Drain

Customers scan the fraudulent code and payment goes through instantly—but to the scammer's bank account. The merchant never knows.

IMPACT: TOTAL_REVENUE_LOSS
03

Zero-Hardware Audit

SatarkMerchant eliminates expensive IoT sensors. Any merchant uses their basic smartphone camera to audit their stand in two taps.

SOLUTION: SMARTPHONE_OPTICAL
